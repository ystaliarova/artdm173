AutoSport Motors
UX Redesign for Auction Platform

My role

  • UX/UI Design
  • Product & Flow Optimization
  • Research & User Behavior Analysis

Client

  • AutoSport Motors

Autosport Motors is a car dealership expanding its business from selling in-stock vehicles to offering cars from American auctions. The platform allows users to browse auction listings, select vehicles, and access additional services like VIN reports.

Expand revenue by introducing auction-based car sales and monetizing services as a licensed intermediary, including paid VIN reports. Improve user experience with clear filters and intuitive navigation to support two key flows: quick VIN report purchase and vehicle discovery.
