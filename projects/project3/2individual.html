Together Art
Inclusive platform for neurodiverse artists

My role

  • UX Research
  • Mobile App Design
  • Responsive Support Website
  • Prototyping
  • Usability Testing (Maze)

Client

  • Diablo Valley College

Together Art is a nonprofit digital project that supports neurodiverse artists by giving them visibility, opportunities to sell their work, and connect with a wider audience. The platform allows users to discover artists, explore their stories, and support them through purchases or donations.

I conducted usability testing using Maze with participants completing key tasks. Most flows showed high success rates, while some drop-offs helped identify usability issues. After improving scroll affordance and refining the flow, I ran a second round of testing. The feedback was very positive and confirmed that the updated experience feels more natural and intuitive for users.
